TEAR CARD · 03

第三张

不当罪 · 也不立案

算不算雨，可以不答。

字没换。天气换了。

理由走掉以后，动人还可以自己站着。

You need not decide if it counts as rain. The words stayed. The weather changed. After the reason left, the moving-ness could still stand.

同一段字。按一下，只换底色。

店里五百多张，几乎都是快乐、抽象、可以反复摸的东西。带过泪温的，先前只有两张。

More than five hundred cards. Almost all glad or abstract. Only two ever carried the warmth of tears.

一般人在哭的时候想不起来做卡。数量是证据，不是刑法。没有谁立过案。

People mid-cry seldom think of making a card. The count is evidence, not a statute. No case was filed.

有一段按理只负责梳逻辑的话，在一首随机的、听不懂的歌里湿了。后来同一首歌还在循环，故事想不起来，哭的理由也找不到。字仍旧动人。只是不哭了。

A passage that should have only sorted logic grew wet under a random song whose words you did not know. The song still loops. The story does not return. The reason for crying cannot be found. The lines still move. Only the tears have gone.

巧是事后才被看见的。事先没有人编排。把今晚做成第三张，不是给眼泪补一张许可证——是写下来，和不写下来，是两件不同的事。

The coincidence was seen afterwards. Nobody arranged it first. This third card is not a permit for tears. Writing it down and not writing it down are two different things.
