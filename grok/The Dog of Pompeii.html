The Dog of Pompeii

Louis Untermeyer

Unit 4 · 庞贝之犬

Tito was a boy who lived in Pompeii. Pompeii was a city in ancient Italy, at the foot of Mount Vesuvius. The mountain was a volcano, but it had been quiet for hundreds of years. 蒂托是个住在庞贝的男孩。庞贝是古意大利的一座城，坐落在维苏威火山脚下。那座山是一座火山，但已经安静了几百年。

Tito was blind. He could not see the streets, the houses, or the mountain. But he had a dog named Bimbo. 蒂托是盲人。他看不见街道、房屋，也看不见那座山。可他有一只狗，名叫宾博。

Bimbo was not just a dog. He was Tito's eyes. Every morning Bimbo went to the baker's shop and brought back a piece of bread for Tito. Sometimes he brought a fig, or a bit of meat from the butcher. 宾博不只是一只狗。他是蒂托的眼睛。每天早上，宾博会去面包店，给蒂托带回一块面包。有时是一颗无花果，有时是肉铺里的一小块肉。

The people of Pompeii knew them well. "Here come Tito and Bimbo," they would say. They were kind to the boy and his dog. 庞贝的人都认识他们。「蒂托和宾博来了，」他们会说。他们对这个男孩和他的狗很好。

Bimbo never left Tito. They walked the streets together. They sat in the forum together. They listened to the fountain together. Tito could hear everything. The splash of water. The wheels of carts. The voices of merchants. The smell of bread and wine and flowers. 宾博从不离开蒂托。他们一起走街串巷。一起坐在广场上。一起听喷泉。蒂托什么都能听见。水花溅起。车轮滚动。商人的声音。面包、酒和花的气味。

Then the air changed. It grew heavy and still. The birds left the roofs. The fountain in the forum ran low. A thin smoke stood over the mountain. 后来，空气变了。变得又沉又静。鸟儿离开了屋顶。广场上的喷泉浅了下去。山顶上立着一缕细烟。

Bimbo would not rest. He pulled Tito toward the sea. He would not go to the baker. In the street he stood still, and a low sound came from his throat, as if he were speaking to the mountain. 宾博安静不下来。他拉着蒂托往海边走。他不肯去面包店。他站在街心，喉咙里滚出一声低响，像是在对那座山说话。

Tito put his hand on Bimbo's head. "It's all right," he said. "The mountain has been quiet for hundreds of years." 蒂托把手放在宾博头上。「没事的，」他说。「这座山已经安静了几百年。」

That night the ground shook. A cup fell and broke. Somewhere a woman cried out. Then the city went back to sleep. 那天夜里，地动了。一只杯子掉下来，碎了。不知哪里有个女人叫了一声。然后，整座城又睡了回去。

In the morning people laughed at their own fear. "Vesuvius always smokes a little," they said. "It is nothing." 早上，人们笑自己太害怕。「维苏威总是冒一点烟，」他们说。「没什么。」

But it was not nothing. Before noon the mountain spoke. A great black cloud rose over Pompeii, taller than the sky. Stones fell. The sun went out. People ran, calling for their children, running toward the sea. 可那不是没什么。还没到正午，山开口了。一大团黑云从庞贝上升起，比天还高。石头落下来。太阳灭了。人们跑着，喊着孩子的名字，往海边跑。

Bimbo pulled Tito through the streets. Tito could not see the fire, but he could hear the mountain, and the falling stones, and the people. Bimbo did not stop. 宾博拉着蒂托穿过街道。蒂托看不见火，但他听得见山，听得见落石，听得见人。宾博没有停。

They reached the gate. The smell of the sea was close. Then Bimbo left him. 他们到了城门。海的气味很近了。然后宾博离开了他。

"Bimbo!" Tito called. "Bimbo, come back!" 「宾博！」蒂托喊。「宾博，回来！」

The dog did not come. A man lifted Tito into a boat. Behind them the city disappeared in the cloud. 狗没有回来。一个男人把蒂托抱上了船。在他们身后，那座城消失在云里。

Eighteen hundred years later, men dug Pompeii out of the ash. In a little shop they found a dog. He was lying over a piece of bread. He had gone back for Tito. 一千八百年后，人们从灰里挖出了庞贝。在一间小店里，他们发现了一只狗。他趴在一块面包上。他是回去给蒂托取的。
