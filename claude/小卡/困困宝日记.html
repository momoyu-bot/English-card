日期

星期一

Monday
天气晴（听说的）Sunny (I heard)
心情(´-ω-`)

今天起得特别早。

I got up really early today.

天还没亮，我就起来了。叠好被子，刷了牙，还喝了一碗热乎乎的粥。

It was still dark. I made my bed, brushed my teeth, and had a bowl of warm porridge.

出门的时候，清醒宝在后面一直喊我，喊了好多好多声。我回过头，跟她说：早安。

On my way out, Awake Bao kept calling me from behind. Many, many times. I turned around and said: good morning.

今天真是有意义的一天。

What a meaningful day.

日期

星期二

Tuesday
天气山顶有风Windy on the mountaintop
心情(˘ω˘)

今天去爬山了。

Today I climbed a mountain.

山不高，软软的。我从山脚一点一点往上爬，爬到了山顶，在山顶插了两根旗杆。一根直直的，一根有点歪。

It wasn’t tall. It was soft. I climbed up little by little. At the top, I put up two flagpoles. One was straight. One leaned a little.

我在山顶待了一整天。风一吹，旗杆就晃一晃。

I stayed on top all day. When the wind blew, the flagpoles swayed.

今天真是有意义的一天觉。

What a meaningful day nap.

✧

那是被子！！！不是山！！！

……山上冷不冷？我又给你加了一床。

That’s a BLANKET!!! Not a mountain!!!

…Is it cold up there? I added one more blanket.

—— 清醒宝— Awake Bao

日期

星期三

Wednesday
天气海边，有海鸥By the sea. Seagulls.
心情(´-ω-`)～

今天去看海了。

Today I went to the sea.

海浪一下，一下，很慢，很好听。我在沙滩上躺了一下午。

The waves came in one by one. Slow and nice. I lay on the beach all afternoon.

海边有一只海鸥，一直叫，叫得特别有精神，好像在说「起来啦」「出来玩嘛」。

A seagull kept calling. It was full of energy. It sounded like “Get up!” and “Come out and play!”

我朝海鸥挥了挥手。

I waved at the seagull.

今天真是有意义的一觉。

What a meaningful nap.

✧

我才不是海鸥！！！

还有，你挥手打到我了！！(๑•̀ㅂ•́)و

I am NOT a seagull!!!

Also, you hit me when you waved!!

—— 清醒宝— Awake Bao

日期

星期四

Thursday
天气夜里，星星很多Night. Lots of stars.
心情(˘ω˘)

我学会了一个新本领：闭着眼睛，也能看见东西。

I learned a new skill: I can see things with my eyes closed.

今天晚上一闭眼，我就看见了好多星星。还有一只会发光的猫，轻手轻脚地走到我床边，看了我好一会儿，帮我掖了掖被角。

Tonight I closed my eyes and saw lots of stars. And a glowing cat. It tiptoed to my bed, looked at me for a long time, and tucked in my blanket.

后来，一只小狐狸举着一块小牌子走过去，牌子上写着：嘘。

Then a little fox walked by with a small sign. The sign said: shh.

这个本领很好用。明天还要用。

This skill is very useful. I’ll use it again tomorrow.

✧

那天晚上我只是路过！！路过而已！！

被角是顺手掖的。顺手！！

I was just passing by that night!! Just passing by!!

I tucked the blanket on the way. ON THE WAY!!

—— 清醒宝— Awake Bao

Awake Bao said she would watch me all day, to see where I go every day.

I said okay. Stay close.

And off we went.

心情(˘ω˘)(˘ω˘)

Today, Awake Bao finally kept up with me.

Today there were four flagpoles on the mountaintop.
